Crypto Investing Basics

Cryptocurrency is digital money that runs on blockchain technology — a decentralized ledger secured by cryptography. Unlike traditional currencies, no single bank or government controls it.


People invest in crypto for potential long-term growth, portfolio diversification, and as a hedge against inflation. Popular assets include Bitcoin (often called “digital gold”), Ethereum (smart-contract platform), and stablecoins like USDT that aim to stay close to $1.


Important: Crypto is highly volatile. Prices can swing dramatically in short periods. Only invest money you can afford to lose, start small, and never share your private keys or seed phrases.
